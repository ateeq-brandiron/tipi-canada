## Our Story

Canada's industrial and energy sectors are entering a period of transformation. Increasing environmental regulations, government incentives, and the growing demand for lower-carbon solutions are reshaping how organizations operate, creating a significant opportunity for commercially viable clean energy solutions.

Turtle Island Power was founded to help organizations navigate that transition through a commercially sustainable business model that delivers practical green hydrogen production, storage, and transportation infrastructure while creating long-term value for investors, industry, and the communities we serve.

As an Indigenous-owned, fully integrated green hydrogen supply chain, we're proving that environmental responsibility and commercial success can move forward together. Guided by Indigenous values of responsibility, respect, and long-term thinking, we're building a repeatable model that strengthens communities, supports industry, and creates lasting opportunity for future generations.

## Ethos

We believe progress should be measured not only by what we build, but by the impact we leave behind.

Every decision is guided by a responsibility to strengthen communities, respect the land, and create opportunities that endure long after the work is complete.

We believe meaningful change happens by bringing people together around practical solutions, not by asking them to choose between progress and responsibility.

## Vision

To restore balance between people, industry, and the land by using innovation and stewardship to leave every community stronger than we found it.

- **An Indigenous-Owned Fully Green Hydrogen Supply Chain**: Indigenous stewardship shapes not only who we are, but how we make decisions, build partnerships, and create long-term value.
- **End-to-End Energy-as-a-Service Model**: From hydrogen production and storage to transportation and delivery, we provide a fully integrated clean energy solution designed to help organizations transition with confidence.
- **Financially Sustainable by Design**: Built on robust economics, government incentives, and commercial viability, our commercially sustainable business model is designed to deliver strong long-term value while demonstrating that environmental responsibility and commercial success can move forward together.
- **Repeatable Community Development Model**: Designed as a scalable blueprint, our approach can be adapted to strengthen Indigenous and rural communities across Canada.

## Value Proposition

Environmental responsibility and commercial success don't have to compete. Through Indigenous-led stewardship, practical innovation, and a commercially sustainable business model, Turtle Island Power empowers hard-to-decarbonize industries to successfully transition to a lower-carbon future while creating long-term value for investors, communities, and industry.

## Value Points

- **Commercially Sustainable Business Model**: Built on sound economics, strategic government incentives, and long-term planning, Turtle Island Power has developed an attractive, commercially sustainable business model designed to deliver strong long-term returns. By ensuring opportunity, investment, and long-term economic benefits remain within the communities where projects are developed, we help build stronger local economies that continue to grow for generations.
